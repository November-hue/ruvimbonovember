Freelance Case Study

The Hoverman — retail ad campaign

The Hoverman is an online electronics store based in South Africa. I was brought on as a freelance graphic designer to create a consistent set of product marketing ads for their website, WhatsApp and social channels — covering ice-making equipment, water dispensers, water pumps and hoverboards.

Client
The Hoverman
Role
Freelance Graphic Designer
Deliverables
5 product ad campaigns
Tools
Layout, iconography, copy

Each ad follows the same design system so the store reads as one brand across products: a fixed logo lock-up, an icon-led feature grid, a consistent accent colour for pricing and discount call-outs, and a shared contact footer. The result is a set of ads a customer can recognise as "The Hoverman" at a glance, whether they're looking at an ice machine or a hoverboard.
